How to Order

Anything in the case can be bought at the counter during open hours. For larger orders, send a pre-order request and pay when you pick up. Please give us 48 hours' notice for bread, pastry and cookie orders and one week for cakes.

All of our items are made in a kitchen that uses wheat, milk, eggs, tree nuts and soy. Tell us about any allergies on the pre-order form and we will do our best to help.
