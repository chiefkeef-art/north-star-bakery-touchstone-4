Welcome to North Star Bakery

Good morning, neighbor! We are a small, family-run bakery on Juniper Street, and we have been baking bread, pastries and cakes by hand since 2012. Grab a warm croissant on your way to work, bring the kids in for a Saturday cinnamon roll, or let us bake for your next celebration.

What We're Known For

  • Slow-rise sourdough

    Our loaves ferment for a full day, which gives them a crackly crust and a soft, tangy inside.

  • Hand-rolled pastries

    Croissants, cinnamon rolls and fruit danishes made with real butter every morning.

  • Celebration cakes

    Birthday, shower and office cakes decorated to order for families and event planners.

  • Easy pre-orders

    Request bread, pastry boxes or cakes ahead of time and pick them up on your way.
