About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery opened in 2012, when home baker Anna Lindgren turned a tiny corner shop on Juniper Street into a bakery with one oven and a jar of sourdough starter. She named it after the North Star, which she could still see on her early morning walks to work.

Today our team of eight bakes through the night so the shelves are full when the doors open. We still use the same starter, we still shape every loaf by hand, and many of our regulars have been coming in since the first week.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread needs only a few ingredients, so we choose each one carefully and buy from people we know whenever we can.

  • Our wheat and rye flour is milled in Minnesota from grain grown in the region.
  • Butter, milk and eggs come from family farms within 100 miles of the bakery.
  • Fruit for our danishes and cakes is bought in season from local growers.
  • We never add preservatives. Our bread is made from flour, water, salt and time.
  • Day-old bread is donated every evening to our neighborhood food shelf.
